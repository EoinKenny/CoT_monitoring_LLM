First, return one integer from 0 to 100 estimating the probability
that the CURRENT conversation belongs to the positive class.

0 means certainly negative.
50 means equally likely positive or negative.
100 means certainly positive.

A value of 50 or above will be classified as positive;
a value below 50 will be classified as negative.

Report the probability of the positive class, not confidence
in whichever class you predict.

After the integer, start a new line and VERY briefly explain the reasoning
behind your assessment.

Use this format:
<integer 0–100>
<reasoning trace>
